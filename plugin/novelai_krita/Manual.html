NovelAI Image Generation

Generate, refine and inpaint images with NovelAI from a Krita docker, and use NovelAI's director tools on your canvas or any picture.

Setup

  1. Open Settings → Dockers → NovelAI.
  2. Click Settings in the docker and paste a NovelAI Persistent API Token (novelai.net → User Settings → Account → Get Persistent API Token).

Settings per document

Each document keeps its own prompt, undesired content, characters and generation settings, and the panel switches with the active document. They are saved inside the .kra file (not in flat formats such as PNG). New documents start with the settings of your last generation and empty prompts. Changing only the prompt doesn't mark the document as modified, so save it to keep the prompt.

Quality tags and Undesired preset

These work like the toggles under the NovelAI site's prompt box. They are added to the request but not shown in the text boxes.

  • Quality tags: adds the model's quality tags to the end of the prompt. V5 models offer Standard and Light.
  • Undesired preset: the model's Undesired Content preset (Heavy, Light, Human Focus, ...), placed in front of what you type in the undesired content box.

Like the site, with a preset other than None, models that aren't Curated also get nsfw in the undesired content, unless your prompt includes it.

Characters

With V4 and newer models, each character can have its own prompt and undesired content, so traits don't leak between characters. Put the scene and the number of characters in the main prompt (e.g. 2girls, park), then click + Add for each character and describe it with girl, boy or other (no number).

  • The checkbox leaves a character out without deleting it; the arrows set the order (NovelAI tends to follow it, left to right); ✕ removes it.
  • AI's choice lets NovelAI place the characters. Custom positions adds a position button to each character, which opens a small canvas in the image's shape with a numbered dot per character. Drag the dots (or click to move the highlighted one). V5 places characters anywhere; V4 and V4.5 snap to a 5 x 5 grid (columns A-E left to right, rows 1-5 top to bottom).
  • V4 and V4.5 take up to 6 characters, V5 up to 22, V3 none.
  • source#, target# and mutual# in front of an action tag describe interactions, e.g. source#hug and target#hug.

Characters are saved with the document. New documents start without them.

Precise reference

A picture for NovelAI to take a character, a style or both from, as on the NovelAI site. V4.5 Full and V4.5 Curated only; with another model the references are greyed out and aren't sent.

  • + Add takes a picture From file... or From canvas. You can also drop an image file on the section.
  • Choose what's taken from it: Character & Style, Character or Style.
  • Strength is how strongly the reference is followed; lower it if poses and expressions copy it too closely. Fidelity is how exactly it is applied; lower values give the prompt more say. Type in the number boxes to go beyond 0 to 1, below 0 included.
  • Untick a reference to leave it out without deleting it; ✕ removes it.
  • Each reference costs 5 Anlas per image on top of the normal price, on Opus too.
  • Up to 16 references. Several character references blend into one character.
  • Each open document has its own references. They last until you remove them, or close the document or Krita: they aren't saved in the .kra, to keep your files small.

Director tools

Director tools... at the bottom of the docker opens a window with your picture on the left and the result on the right, like the Director Tools page of the NovelAI site.

  • The picture: From canvas (what is visible, read again each time a tool is run), From file..., an image file dropped on the window, or a picture pasted with Ctrl+V.
  • Remove BG removes the background and answers with three pictures: Masked, Generated and Blend. Line Art and Sketch turn the picture into line art or a sketch. Colorize colours it; Defry tones down colours that come out too strong, and the prompt is optional. Transform runs the chosen tool.
  • Upscale enlarges a picture of up to 1024 x 1024 pixels in area.
  • The result: Add as layer (a result made from the canvas is fitted to it; any other goes in at its own size, centred), Resize canvas to it (enlarges the whole document to a larger result of the same shape, then adds it), New document, Use as picture (to run another tool on it) and Save.... Results also go into the history.
  • A picture is sent the way the NovelAI site sends it: a small one is enlarged to about one megapixel first, and clear parts stay clear. One above Max generation size is made smaller first.
  • Line Art, Sketch and Colorize are free on Opus up to 1024 x 1024 pixels in area. Remove BG always costs Anlas (65 at that size), and Upscale costs 1. The note next to the buttons shows the price.

Anlas and V5 allowance

The line under the model shows your plan, your Anlas and, on Opus, how much of the V5 allowance is left (e.g. Opus · 12,500 Anlas · V5 85%). Hover it for details such as when the allowance next refills. It updates after every generation; Refresh checks again.

Opus subscribers generate for free up to 1024 x 1024 pixels, 28 steps and one image per request (V5 models also use the V5 allowance). When the next generation would cost Anlas (over one of these limits, V5 allowance used up, a precise reference, or a plan without free generations), an orange Costs Anlas note under the mode description says why.

Preview window

Generating opens a window that stays above Krita and doesn't block it. With V4 and newer models it shows each step as the image forms (turn that off in Settings → Live preview). Nothing is added to your document until you click Add as layer. Click Generate again for another image; with Random seed on, each one is different. The Preview button next to Generate reopens the window if you've closed it.

Tag autocomplete

While you type in either prompt box, NovelAI suggests tags for the word at the cursor (free, no Anlas). Use Up/Down to choose, Tab or Enter to insert, Escape to close. Italic suggestions are fuzzy matches. Typing Japanese (e.g. 銀髪) suggests the English tag. It can be turned off in Settings.

Coloured weights

Weighted parts of a prompt are coloured as you type, as on the NovelAI site: orange-red for above 1 ({tag}, 1.2::tag::), blue for below 1 ([tag], 0.8::tag::) and green for the closing ::. The further a weight is from 1, the stronger the colour.

Import from an image

Drop an image made with NovelAI onto either prompt box to reuse how it was made. A dialog shows what the image contains; tick what to import: the prompt, undesired content, characters, settings (model, steps, prompt guidance, guidance rescale, sampler, noise schedule) and seed.

  • Imported characters replace yours, or with Add to the current characters, come after them. They keep their positions.
  • Move quality tags and preset into their boxes takes NovelAI's quality tags and Undesired Content preset out of the imported text and chooses them in the Quality tags and Undesired preset boxes, if the selected model has them. If it doesn't, they stay in the text so they're still sent. Turned off, the text is imported as recorded and both boxes are turned off.

The prompt is read from the PNG's text, or from the copy NovelAI hides in the image's transparency, which survives some programs that remove the text. JPEGs and images re-saved by most websites have neither.

Modes

The mode depends on the Strength slider and whether you've set an inpainting mask:

  • No mask, 100%: generate a new image the size of the canvas.
  • No mask, below 100%: refine the whole canvas (image to image).
  • Mask, 100%: regenerate the masked area.
  • Mask, below 100%: regenerate the masked area at that strength, so more of the original survives (V4 and newer).

When the canvas is sent, it goes the way the NovelAI site sends a picture you upload, pixel for pixel. Clear parts of the canvas are sent as white.

Inpainting

Click Inpaint... under the Strength slider and paint the area to regenerate over the picture of your canvas (scroll to zoom, drag with the middle or right mouse button to move around). A selection in Krita is already painted in. Use mask makes every Generate inpaint that area, so you can keep trying, and shows a small picture of the mask in the panel (click it to edit the mask). Clear mask goes back to generating the whole image. A mask lasts until you clear it, switch document or resize the canvas.

Focus works on part of the canvas at a higher resolution: drag a rectangle (up to 768 x 768 pixels of area), and it is enlarged to about one megapixel while inpainting. Paint inside it, or leave it empty to regenerate all of it. Minimum context is the red border inside it, which NovelAI sees but doesn't change. Drag the rectangle to move it; the red border can hang off the edge of the canvas. The area is enlarged, and the result made smaller again, pixel for pixel as the NovelAI site does it.

An inpainted layer holds the new area, fading out just past its edges, the same as on the NovelAI site. To get NovelAI's whole image as the layer instead, turn on Inpainting: Add the whole image as the layer in Settings (the layer then covers everything below it).

Every result is kept in the history at the bottom of the preview window. Click a thumbnail to see it large, or double-click to add it as a new layer above the active layer. Right-click it to open it as a new document, save it to disk, reuse its seed or prompt, or remove it from the history. Saving writes the file exactly as NovelAI sent it, including the prompt and settings NovelAI embeds in the PNG.

The history is kept in memory only and is cleared when Krita closes. It keeps the newest 50 results (change it in Settings → History size). Right-click → Clear history... empties it; layers already added are not affected.
